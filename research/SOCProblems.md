# SOC Problems

## Problem Statement

Modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs) generate thousands of security alerts every day. Security analysts spend significant time manually investigating alerts, correlating related events, and identifying whether they represent a real security incident or a false positive.

This process is often repetitive, time-consuming, and mentally exhausting.

Current SIEM platforms provide alerts, but they do not always provide sufficient context, explain relationships between events, or clearly describe the complete attack story.

As a result, analysts must switch between multiple tools, threat intelligence platforms, and documentation to understand what actually happened.

---

# Saqr Solution

Saqr-JO is designed to assist SOC analysts by:

* Correlating related security events.
* Building a complete attack timeline.
* Mapping attacker behavior to the MITRE ATT&CK framework.
* Enriching indicators using Threat Intelligence sources.
* Generating explainable AI-assisted incident summaries.
* Supporting analyst decisions without replacing human judgment.

---

# Engineering Principle

Saqr never makes security decisions on behalf of the analyst.

The analyst always remains responsible for the final decision.

AI is used only to assist, explain, and accelerate investigations.
